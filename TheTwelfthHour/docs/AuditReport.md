# Second-pass technical audit — 19 October 2026

Audited baseline: `d59c4ebb240e02cc3dd6f377f7ba8a5361f00d6d`. Target: Civilization V BNW, Community Patch DLL v151, official commit `44c1e14ff75d80c1dba5c8a71e8289439a53aafb`.

## Assessment

The native dummy-building yield design and six-played-turn staging architecture are sound for the supported ordinary sequential single-player path. The second pass found a save-API calling-contract mismatch hidden by the original mock, plus recoverable-error and notification weaknesses. Those paths are repaired without changing SQL, gameplay values, persistence keys, artwork, the cycle, or other civilizations. The civilization is **ready for normal gameplay testing**, with actual sixth-turn yield settlement and native save/autosave acceptance the first priorities. It is not certified by native execution: **Civ V was not launched in this audit**.

Ten relevant CP source files were freshly fetched from the pinned commit, independently reread, and checked with 43 executable source contracts. Installed Firaxis scenario code and the read-only save database schema were also inspected. See [SourceAudit.json](SourceAudit.json) and [SaveApiAudit.json](SaveApiAudit.json). No finding below represents an observed native game crash or a claimed native test pass.

## Independent turn and autosave trace

Pinned [CvPlayer.cpp](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/44c1e14ff75d80c1dba5c8a71e8289439a53aafb/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvPlayer.cpp), [CvGame.cpp](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/44c1e14ff75d80c1dba5c8a71e8289439a53aafb/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvGame.cpp) and [CustomMods.h](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/44c1e14ff75d80c1dba5c8a71e8289439a53aafb/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CustomMods.h) establish:

1. `setTurnActive(true)` resets end-turn state and calls `doTurn`; normal human economics runs through `doTurnPostDiplomacy`. City `doTurn`/Production and then player research precede `PlayerDoTurn`.
2. `CheckPlayerTurnDeactivate` requires an alive active player, ending/AI eligibility, processed automoves, and completed activity. It calls `setTurnActive(false)` **before** activating the next sequential player. During that call, `PlayerDoneTurn(int playerID)` runs behind `EVENTS_PLAYER_TURN`, enabled by the existing Twelfth SQL.
3. Consequently staging at the end of played turn 5 precedes the economic pass entering 6; staging at the end of 6 removes the effects for 7. The same reasoning applies to 11/12/13. The global turn counter increments once between rounds. `(raw-start+1)%6==0` is unaffected by speed and keeps the first five played turns normal in an advanced-era start.
4. The first-alive player's single-player end-turn autosave runs at the **beginning** of `setTurnActive(false)`, before the active flag changes and before PlayerDoneTurn. Its database and dummies therefore correctly contain the *pre-staging* state.
5. `CvPlayer::Serialize` stores `m_bTurnActive`, `m_bEndTurn`, and `m_bAutoMoves`; it does **not** store `m_bProcessedAutoMoves`. `Read` restores state/active-player counts without invoking economic `doTurn`. In the ordinary resumed path, `updateMoves` processes restored automoves, rebuilds the processed flag, and `CheckPlayerTurnDeactivate` invokes the missing deactivation/Done event. The new fixture explicitly follows these relevant conditions instead of pretending the autosave already contains the stage.

This supports ordinary autosave resumption; it is **not** proof of all closed-engine loading/UI/mission behavior. Automove cancellation, delayed diplomacy, unusual scenario control, and actual engine autosave restoration still need native tests. The event is not unconditional: already-inactive players do not emit another Done hook, an eliminated player need not finish a turn, PBEM has a separate activation path, and another mod could disable the required option. These are not confirmed missing-Chime defects in supported sequential single-player play. No speculative pre-harvest event or arbitrary yield compensation was added.

Founding/acquisition changes rates and dummies; it does not explicitly grant yields or call research/Production from Lua. A city founded after the sixth-turn economic pass cannot gain a second ordinary pass merely from receiving a dummy; ending 6 removes the temporary effect before 7. Capture's ABI is old owner, capital flag, x, y, **new owner**, population, conquest, plus CP great-work arguments. The fifth argument is correctly used. `CityCaptureComplete` follows native new-city reconstruction. Production and gold/faith purchase hooks follow `CreateBuilding`; investment-only purchases without a completed building correctly do not qualify. Native capture/founding and unusual script-created cities still require gameplay acceptance.

## Persistence and cross-save isolation

`OpenSaveData()` accesses the game's `Civ5SavedGameDatabase.db` (`SimpleValues(Name TEXT Primary Key, Value VARIANT)` on the installed engine). It is shared among mods **within that saved game**; it is distinct from the named, per-user `OpenUserData` stores under `ModUserData`. Firaxis scenarios use per-save initialization sentinels and persistent turn markers through this API. The author of [TableSaverLoader](https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/tablesaverloader-for-persisting-lua-table-data-through-game-save-load.442249/) describes the database being packed into the Civ5Save and restored on load, including quicksave/autosave handling.

Thus, under the API's savegame contract, a same-numbered turn in game A cannot suppress a notification or stage game B. Loading an older save restores that save's values, not a monotonic account-wide ledger. Current keys already include a unique civilization/version/player prefix, so **no game GUID, reset, renaming or migration is warranted**. The fix retains existing legitimate values and unrelated mods' keys. A snapshot from before notification delivery may legitimately deliver the message when that earlier timeline is replayed; a snapshot made after delivery must not repeat it.

The native engine implementation of this provider is outside the published CP DLL. Installed script/schema evidence, the provider author's documented container behavior, and realistic model rewinds support this conclusion; **an actual A/B game switch and native serializer round trip remain unperformed**. Cross-save contamination is not confirmed. External database locks or an engine/provider malfunction are outside what this module can prove or repair with invented identifiers.

## Real yield calculation audit

Pinned [CvCity.cpp](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/44c1e14ff75d80c1dba5c8a71e8289439a53aafb/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvCity.cpp), `CvBuildingClasses.cpp`, and `CvPlayer.cpp` show:

- `SetNumRealBuilding(..., true)` calls `processBuilding`; `Building_YieldModifiers` changes native yield-rate modifiers, which refresh cached static yields immediately. The flat Clocktower dummy contributes through base building yields. These affect real stored construction and research, not just tooltips.
- The ordinary city calculation multiplies base yield in hundredths by `max(0,100 + ordinary + item + Golden Age + Chime + other additive modifiers)`, dividing by 100 with integer truncation. Unit/building/wonder/project item selection feeds the normal current-production modifier. Neither dummy bypasses those rules.
- Processes, certain converted yields/food Production, and trade-route yields use native post-modifier positions. A city modifier is not a promise to multiply every final displayed component by 1.75 or 1.50. The implementation correctly preserves those established rules; it does not double-count existing infrastructure or scale external player-wide Science sources.
- Multiplicative modifiers occur after modified base and post-modifier yields; trade is then added. Active anarchy, resistance and razing return zero from the rate calculation. City turn processing can decrement resistance before harvesting, so a city leaving resistance may produce normally on that boundary.
- `doProduction` credits the native Production rate plus stored overflow/feature Production; prior overflow is not multiplied by the current Chime again. `doResearch` adds ordinary Science and saved research overflow before crediting team progress; overflow is not separately boosted. The native no-Science option remains respected.
- Municipal Clocktower retains exactly +1 additional base Culture permanently. Its +2 base Production is conditional and participates in normal modifiers: with base 20 and no other modifiers, a tower city has `(20+2)*1.75=38.50` on Chime, while base Science 30 becomes 45. These are illustrative arithmetic results, not a recorded game measurement.

New CP-derived arithmetic models cover all construction categories, additive infrastructure/item/Golden Age modifiers, post-modifier and trade positioning, suppression, zero base yields, overflow and hundredth rounding. Source contracts verify the expressions/order independently. **Neither is an actual stored native queue/research-delta test.** No balance or SQL change was necessary.

## Unit, Workshop and lifecycle assessment

The active-schema comparison checks every inherited scalar and direct companion-row multiset, preserving costs, maintenance, technology/resource prerequisites, specialist slots/yields, class rules, CP additions, original Pikeman promotions and upgrades. It compares exact multiplicities, so cloning direct rows does not duplicate them. The original Workshop/Pikeman and all preexisting rows remain unchanged. Culture is additive even when the baseline already supplies Culture.

The native promotion path adds `MovesChange` once to extra moves. The Pendulum promotion contributes exactly +1; it does not change the scalar base Moves. Normal land movement therefore increases by one; embarkation/linked-unit rules retain native behavior. `FriendlyLandsModifier=15` feeds the common melee strength routine called by both attack and defense. It checks the relevant battle plot's `IsFriendlyTerritory`; an attack from friendly land into ordinary enemy land does not receive this common modifier just because its origin is friendly. `FriendlyLandsAttackModifier=0` prevents another +15 on attack. Ranged/general native paths are also preserved. Upgrade transfer retains eligible promotions with `LostWithUpgrade=0`; the custom promotion lacks the nonretainable conversion/embarkation flags and does not become a ranged-only/melee-only promotion on combat-class changes. Actual combat previews and an upgrade are still required in-game.

Both dummies remain unbuildable/unpurchasable, maintenance-free, hidden, noncapturable, and normalized to 0/1. Founding/capture/gift/liberation synchronize affected owners; foreign recipients are scrubbed. Dead/zero-city/invalid players are safe. Removed cities are never retained as userdata; deleted/razed-city fixture cases are covered. Clocktower completion/purchase uses the verified post-creation hook, sale now uses post-removal state synchronously, and scripted removal retains the dirty-event fallback plus synchronous turn-end repair. Transient faults get one immediate retry; persistent errors are bounded/logged and must not be described as guaranteed recoverable before harvest.
