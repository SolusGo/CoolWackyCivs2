# At the First Threshold

There shall be no second city. For the first shall have no end.

First Architect, the seven crossings have placed their seals beside yours. Their people bring the memory of a flood, the tools of their trades and a question that stone alone cannot answer: who will keep the promises made between strangers?

Raise the Heart of the World around that question. Give its wards a hearing, its Surveyors a road home and its Registry the courage to preserve an inconvenient truth. Let distant households share a common citizenship without surrendering the customs that make their neighborhoods their own.

The fields beyond the hills will need hands. The islands will need crossings. Every new boundary will enlarge the work already owed. Build patiently, defend those entrusted to you and leave room at the table for the person your finest drawing has overlooked.

The foundations are laid. How far can one city's promise travel?
