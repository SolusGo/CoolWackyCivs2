Development manifest checks identified stale hashes after source edits. Rebuilding after the edits resolved the mismatches. Pre-commit review also caught Windows CRLF output in the generated Lua configuration and leader scene; their generators now force LF so checked-in bytes match manifest hashes. An unused legacy trade policy table was replaced after pinned source inspection showed its compile guard was absent; the working native scalar is used and its broader yield behavior is documented. No failed check is represented as native proof.

All preexisting civilization gameplay sources and shipped art are preserved. Timing-only reports regenerated by existing suites are restored to their original committed versions; Jin's new validation, benchmark and source reports are retained. The collection UUID is unchanged, version advances from 15 to 16, and Jin's SQL follows the entire released inventory.

The final native API check covers `PushOrder`'s numeric force argument and explicit builder IDs when placing improvements. Strict behavioral mocks exercise the queue contract and saved improvement builder; the final thirteen Jin scenarios pass with these bindings.
