# Red artwork provenance

Two original paintings were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-16, then copied into this repository. The first named-character generation was rejected; the successful leader scene is an original visual homage. No commercial Pokémon artwork or music files are included.

`RedOnSilver.png` prompt: “Original hand-painted widescreen static leader scene for a Civilization V fan mod. A quiet mountain adventurer, adult man in a red cap with a white front, red and white outdoors jacket, dark shirt, blue jeans and backpack, stands on a snowy mountain summit. Serious calm expression, dark hair, hand touching cap. Three-quarter figure prominently on right half with readable face. Frozen mountain ridges fill left half, wind blows snow, silver-blue dusk with warm cinematic rim light, solitude and mastery. Original character illustration, richly painted strategy game aesthetic. No text, UI, watermarks or other characters. Landscape 16:9.”

`SilverDawn.png` prompt: “Original Civilization V Dawn of Man loading artwork. A tiny but imposing lone adult mountain adventurer in a red cap, red-and-white jacket, jeans and backpack stands with his back to the viewer on a snow-covered rocky summit. He overlooks a vast frozen landscape of towering ridges, snow, and distant valleys beneath silver-blue dawn light. The figure occupies only a small part of the image near lower right; immense mountains communicate solitude, mastery and legend. Cinematic hand-painted strategy-game illustration, elegant rich texture. No text, logos, creatures, watermark or UI. Wide landscape 4:3.”

`tools/prepare_red_art.py` produces all DDS variants, the Kanto/Johto-inspired map, symbolic identity/rank/building/project/cap/flag artwork, and `InsigniaPreview.png`. These symbols extend the collection's existing deterministic medallion style. The shared `standardize_icons.py` applies the collection's gold rims/padding from retained PNG masters. DXT5 and RGBA outputs have full mip chains. Run the preparation script only when regenerating sources; regular package builds consume committed textures and retained masters.
