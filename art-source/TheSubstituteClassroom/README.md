# Original Classroom art

`MrGarvey.png` was generated with the **built-in imagegen tool**, then copied into this repository as the retained project source. No existing civilization artwork was replaced. `tools/prepare_garvey_art.py` prepares the game formats, portrait crop and deterministic clipboard emblem, monitor shield, office doorway, floor plan and attendance blackboard. `Icon.png`, `Portrait.png`, `Objects.png` and `Alpha.png` are unframed masters; the collection's IconAtlasMasters stores the gold-finisher inputs. `Preview.png` is a source/contact preview; final atlas framing is applied by build_mod.py.

Exact generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Civilization V custom civilization static leader and loading screen, landscape 16:9. Paint Mr. Garvey from Key and Peele's Substitute Teacher: stern middle-aged Black substitute teacher, close cropped hair, neat moustache, square glasses, pale short-sleeved dress shirt, dark tie, gripping an attendance clipboard. He stands slightly right of center in an ordinary American classroom, imposing absolute authority, eyebrow raised with comic intensity. Deep green chalkboard behind him, chalk dust, wooden desk, classroom door, warm late-afternoon light. Polished painterly strategy-game illustration, grounded and humorous, rich green, cream and muted gold, detailed face. Waist-up figure, face unobstructed and large enough for portrait crop. No students, no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no border.

Artwork is a new artistic depiction inspired by the fictional comedy character. No sketch footage, audio or custom 3D model is bundled.
