# Urgal artwork prompts

The hardening pass adds `PotionUI32.png` and `URGAL_PotionUI32.dds`, compiled deterministically from the existing bottle symbol by `prepare_urgal_art.py`. It removes the medallion background, preserves the purple glass/cork silhouette and fits it on a transparent 32px canvas with a complete legacy DDS mip chain. No new image generation or external dependency was used for the UI icon.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-26. Original PNGs live in this directory; `tools/prepare_urgal_art.py` compiles the game textures. The potion emblem, monochrome alpha, promotion badges and illustrative arena map are deterministic collection-style graphics.

## Urgal.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Civilization V static leader and Dawn of Man painting for an original Minecraft MCPVP Hardcore Games civilization called The Urgal. Create a wide 16:9 cinematic strategy-game oil painting: a determined block-inspired adult human warrior, rugged dark hair, iron chestplate and leather straps, holds a small glass bottle of glowing magenta Strength II potion near his chest; iron sword lowered in other hand. He is the composed leader of an army preparing a decisive charge, standing at a wooden brewing table at the edge of a blocky forest battlefield at dawn. Strong readable face in left third, upper body completely in frame, dramatic purple potion light, warm dawn, darker distant army silhouettes on right. Sophisticated painterly voxel-inspired world, convincing materials, evocative tactical preparation. No horns, no orc, no text, no logos, no watermark, no UI. Important: this is a Minecraft kit player, not the fantasy Urgal species.
