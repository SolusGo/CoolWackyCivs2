# Implementation and engine boundaries

`MCP_Core.lua` creates the isolated `MapModData.MCPVP` namespace and includes persistence, navigation, matches, kits, AI and events. Only `MCP_ServerBrowser.xml` is an InGameUIAddin. Its Lua renderer reads existing state and submits validated explicit commands; rendering never creates records, runs pathfinding or grants rewards. One-shot post-native dispatch clears its update callback before doing queued work. No idle polling, timers or custom DLL are used.

## Database and native interfaces

SQL clones the active America's identity defaults, Washington's leader defaults, the active Scout and Barracks, then all relevant available unit/building companion tables. The only Scout scalar gameplay change is strength 6; the Hub's bonuses are additive. Scoped `MCPVP` identifiers and unique VFS basenames prevent collisions. The builder loads Shared compatibility tables first, before alphabetically ordered civilization modules; this is necessary because McPvP sorts before Shared. Existing native types are not rewritten.

Promotions provide Veteran/Final Battle/remembrance XP, Grace defense, kit indicators, Kangaroo mobility/defense, Flag defense and status indicators. Capital Culture uses five hidden binary-weighted buildings, giving an exact 0–18 total. Sabotage uses one noncapturable hidden building with verified `Building_YieldModifiers` rows. The union of all active attackers maintains it; it never stacks and ownership changes remove stale effects.

Source audits use official CP v151 commit `44c1e14ff75d80c1dba5c8a71e8289439a53aafb`, retained in ignored `tools/reference/`. The audit verifies 83 invoked native method names, critical methods on their exact native objects, both complete combat callback argument lists, route-query return fields, damage forwarding and movement flag semantics. Native `SetXY` and `CanMoveOrAttackInto` require integer optional flags; `SetMadeAttack` and `IsInvisible` use actual booleans. BNW/CP's Information Era is **ERA_FUTURE**, confirmed by CP's `INFORMATION_ERA` define.

In both `CombatResult` and `CombatEnded`, argument 3 is damage received by the attacker and argument 8 is damage received by the defender. Native local names are misleading: `attackerDamage = getDamageInflicted(BATTLE_UNIT_DEFENDER)`. Arguments 4 and 9 are cumulative damage totals, not remaining HP. Viper records an eligible positive defender hit before resolution, correlates completion by both participant IDs and turn, confirms positive resolved damage and actual target survival, then applies poison through deferred dispatch. Duplicate/unrelated callbacks cannot pay another application. Net wounds are not used to infer a hit, because post-combat healing can offset genuine damage. Poison remains one saved block with exactly two five-damage ticks.

`UnitConverted(oldOwner,newOwner,oldID,newID,isUpgrade)` fires before the source unit is killed. A same-owner upgrade rebinds its serial, creation turn, Veteran identity, kit and outstanding cooldowns to the successor. Native conversion does not copy ScriptData, so the handoff explicitly tags the successor. The source's later prekill cannot delete its successor record. Non-upgrade or ownership-changing conversions clear transferable rights. Poison is explicitly transferred only across same-owner upgrades.

CP's `OnlyDefensive` promotion blocks melee, but `IsCanAttackRanged` ignores it. Flag carriers also exhaust ordinary attacks when their status is applied; the verified `MustAbortAttack` hook guards combat resolution, including Blitz/ranged edge cases. Ordinary upgrade/gift/delete commands are vetoed by `CanDoCommand`; `UnitCanHaveAnyUpgrade` blocks other normal upgrade paths. Forced conversion safely loses flags and infiltration.

## Persistence and idempotency

Length-delimited, deterministic, bounded table encoding is stored inside `MCPSTATE1` player ScriptData. Unit records use an owner-scoped monotonically increasing serial plus native ID and creation turn; copied tags cannot impersonate a different current native unit. City references use plot coordinates, original owner and founding turn, checked against current ownership. Current/lifetime Legacy, cycle boundaries, all ledgers, Feast status, flags, cooldowns, missions, debuffs and remembrance are saved after changes. Poison resides in the affected unit's `MCPPOISON1` block, with remaining ticks, source and last processed owner turn. Unrelated ScriptData is preserved.

Claims are committed before native rewards, training is recorded once per unit, city raid/mission ledgers reset only at cycle boundaries, and kill identities are deduplicated per cycle. Hardcore ordinary kill Legacy is capped at 15 and the active-Feast battle bonus at 10. Barbarians, city-states and zero-cost military types cannot supply kill Legacy. Kills originate from actual native prekill events; no artificial opponents are spawned. Claims and all economic rewards work for rival Feast claimants.

Native public Lua does not reliably identify a third-party script summoning a permanent ordinary paid unit type. Such a unit can be a normal war casualty. No reward is granted merely for arbitrary unit creation, conversion or a repeated hit on a living unit. Other mods that fabricate native training/kill events or rewrite ScriptData remain outside the guarantee.

The Feast search runs at most three times during Hardcore preparation: initially, after Grace Period and at the announcement deadline. Each search explores up to 768 reachable capital-region nodes, two rival regions of up to 384 nodes each, and up to six native route queries plus 384 shared fallback nodes for practical military arrival. Candidate distance remains 4–16 tiles; the region may detour out to radius 22. Three reachable neighboring approaches, land-area consistency and non-overlap remain requirements. Rival route cost, neutral territory, detour cost and balanced approach distances affect scoring. At most eight shortlisted sites and two nearest available McPvP military units per site are probed. If no military unit exists, a legal capital route permits future trainees and rival competition. No full-map traversal is used. Existing saved Feasts are preserved rather than rerolled.

`MCP_Navigation.lua` reads CP's `GeneratePath(destination,maxTurns)` as a table of X/Y/RemainingMovement/Turn rows; an empty table means no route. The binding uses `MOVEFLAG_IGNORE_STACKING_SELF`, so every returned tile is additionally checked for occupancy, revelation, domain, native `CanMoveThrough`, territory and city access. Only adjacent noncombat native orders are issued. The weighted Dijkstra fallback uses native `MovementCost(unit,fromPlot,remainingMoves)` and is limited to 192 expanded nodes per plan, radius 20 and 32 steps. AI players share six native queries, 640 fallback expansions and four new plans per global turn, including repeated callbacks. Upcoming mode selection has a separate two-query/192-node cycle budget. Per-unit movement continuations are triggered only by actual native arrival events, use remaining movement, and stop after eight orders that turn. There is no idle update loop.

Carrier routes penalize native `GetPlotDanger` and reject a currently predicted lethal tile. Infiltrators consume AI movement while their mission is active; human movement rules are unchanged. Routes replan on changed occupancy/access or unexpected position, invalid city references and cycle/Feast changes. Two native orders without position progress cause a three-scaled-turn target cooldown. A completely searched but unreachable target also cools down; budget exhaustion is deferred, with a short throttle only after repeated exhausted searches. Native orders belonging to other systems are preserved; only our own queued step is popped when abandoned. Units rotate through planning opportunities. All navigation caches are transient and keyed by native ID, creation turn and existing unit serial. They are not added to the saved schema and can be discarded safely on load.

Ability targeting scans only its one/two-tile radius, AI city targets are limited to four nearby visible cities, and ordinary foreign movement is ignored unless it reaches a live Feast. McPvP units reconcile on their owner turn and relevant events; active poison only scans affected owners. Debuff reconciliation scans cities once per global turn or after city ownership changes. No per-frame map scan exists.

## Deliberate translations and limitations

- Static original geometric art, original insignia and native Scout battlefield/unit-flag art replace custom animated art. No external copyrighted textures, music or voice lines are required. Native diplomacy supplies generic fallback situations beyond the authored greeting, war, peace and defeat text.
- A Feast's notification coordinates and Locate button are the map indication. It is an objective recorded on a plot, not a new permanent improvement that could destroy an existing improvement. A unit must remain at the tile for the deferred native check; passing through it is insufficient.
- Placement checks bounded legal land routes and practical unit arrival, rather than land-area membership alone. Budget limits, future border/unit changes and approximate rival travel costs can still make a site difficult or decline a distant but reachable site. Competition is encouraged, not guaranteed; no-site matches continue without a fake victory.
- Endermage uses the victim's native destination legality and an empty adjacent tile. It never forces a stack, enters a city or bypasses impassable terrain. Stomper and Thor also exclude units occupying cities. Direct damage uses native death handling rather than artificial combat.
- Flag return requires an owned city/working-city tile. Death loses the flag and notifies both sides; there is no separate dropped-flag object. A city's raid remains consumed after failure, deliberately preventing repeated flag farming.
- Poison always has two fixed damage ticks, rather than adding ticks on slow speeds. Ability damage and XP awards are unscaled. Economic/Culture awards scale by the actual speed and era, as documented in the guide.
- Native routes and bounded detours improve objective movement, but this is not a full strategic or globally optimal planner. Searches deliberately avoid occupied tiles and foreign city centers. Danger and fallback travel times are estimates; the game's tactical AI may change orders, and movement animations can defer continuation. No free scores or fake enemies compensate for this.
- The human Browser submits gameplay commands to the owning addin, using the established collection single-player architecture. Network commands are rejected; multiplayer/hotseat are unsupported and unverified.
- Corrupt/newer McPvP state is preserved and refused, with Lua.log diagnostics. Native engine exceptions can interrupt a reward after a claim is committed; it will not pay twice.

All systems remain active after Legacy Unforgotten. No calendar-year test, collapse, unit deletion, arbitrary Legacy spending or permanent native building/yield alteration is used.
