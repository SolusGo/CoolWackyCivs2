## Reproducible economic timing test

1. Use a new modded single-player BNW game with CP151 only (not Vox Populi) and this collection. Enable LoggingEnabled and FireTuner in the game's config as appropriate. Back up configuration and saves. Select Maren, found a native coastal city and prepare an owned Trireme/Cutter inside its city center. Remove other yield modifiers where practical; hold population, citizen assignment, trade routes, policies, research target and city buildings fixed. Choose a research target that will not finish during the comparison; avoid Gold deficits and anarchy.
2. In FireTuner choose the **LANTERN_Harbors** Lua context and run `include("LANTERN_TimingProbe")`. This imported module is optional, read-only, not automatically loaded, and prints `LANTERN_TIMING` and `LANTERN_CITY` records to Lua.log. It records start status and the later PlayerDoTurn boundary, treasury/culture stocks, Science rate, per-city yields and both counts. Stop with `LANTERN_StopTimingProbe()`.
3. Make paired pre-boundary saves: A starts with no guard and dummy counts 0; B has a guard in the center but the same counts 0. Advance exactly ONE owner-turn boundary. The status event must occur for the human, untagged, while IsTurnActive is true. The conditional counts must change before native settlement, and B must gain the expected additional Science research progress and Culture in that immediate boundary. Adding the Signal Lighthouse in both branches must yield the extra conditional Gold in B, with the same permanent +1 Culture in both. Account for the ordinary native modifiers and complete all comparisons before another owner-turn. Record research progress with Teams[player:GetTeam()]:GetTeamTechs():GetResearchProgress(currentTech), not Science rate alone.
4. Repeat the reverse transition: preexisting counts 1, remove the ship before next start. The immediate following settlement must lose the conditional increments. Inspect stocks and research progress, not merely the top-panel rate. A notification printed before PlayerDoTurn by itself is insufficient: the calculations could already have run before both callbacks.
5. Test moving in and out during the active turn, saving/reloading both after entry and departure, repeated status notifications, fresh contexts, ordinary save restoration and the initial owner-turn. No mid-turn dummy changes or extra payouts are allowed. Capture with either foreign or Lantern recipient must show both counts zero immediately and normal reevaluation at its next genuine owner start.
6. Repeat with a scenario/debug AI Lantern player to establish event coverage beyond human selection. Verify synchronous dispatch under the deployed Civ V executable; stop acceptance if the candidate UI notification is deferred. Source publication ordering cannot replace this step.
