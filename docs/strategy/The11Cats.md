# The 11 Cats - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../The11Cats/README.md)

**Leader:** Austin  
**Playstyle:** Peaceful city specialization through a persistent household  
**Suggested victories:** Culture; Science or Diplomacy through healthy specialized cities

Place companions in cities where their resident effects and compatible bonds support a coherent economy. The household is a long-term allocation system; the final family count is a narrative milestone, not an extra yield package.

## Opening priorities

Establish safe food, Culture and expansion for future homes. When a companion arrives, inspect its actual resident effects and settle it into a useful available home instead of leaving it travelling unnecessarily.

## Develop the engine

Use the Homes and Bonds views to plan small groups within the resident limit. Combine complementary effects with real local infrastructure. Rehome deliberately when conditions change, respecting recovery, arrival and action restrictions.

## Convert the advantage

Convert mature homes and longer genuine WLTKD celebrations into the infrastructure needed for a normal cultural or diplomatic victory. Keep travelers protected and maintain safe arrivals when a companion is recovering.

## Pros

- Several distinct resident effects allow meaningful city specialization.
- Family Bonds reward deliberate household composition.
- Longer genuine WLTKD celebrations support a peaceful growth plan.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Each city has limited resident capacity.
- Companions arrive through Era chapters rather than all at the start.
- The household does not supply a general early military breakthrough.

## Playing against this civ

- Pressure promising homes and the routes used by travelling companions.
- Force emergency military investment before peaceful specialization has matured.
- Contest the ordinary cultural and diplomatic objectives rather than expecting the family milestone itself to win the game.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming Austin is an ordinary movable cat unit.
- Expecting eleven residents in one city or unlimited bond stacking.
- Treating Everyone Is Home as an extra yield reward or a standard victory.

## Who pressures whom

Named custom-civilization matchups are conditional mechanical analysis, not tested win rates. All custom civs are human-only and multiplayer is unsupported; these comparisons are for theorycraft or explicitly configured scenarios, not ordinary random AI opponents. General counterplay applies to normal single-player games.
