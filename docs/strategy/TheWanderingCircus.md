# The Wandering Circus - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../TheWanderingCircus/README.md)

**Leader:** Ringmaster Aurelio  
**Playstyle:** Worker logistics and recurring local cultural festivals  
**Suggested victories:** Culture; Science or Diplomacy from developed cities

Use Carnival Crews for real improvements, then bring them through eligible city centres to stage festivals. A good route turns one worker's travel into several timed local benefits without leaving the countryside unfinished.

## Opening priorities

Develop food and production with the faster-moving Crew. Plan its route past the capital and future cities. A festival is useful, but the ordinary improvements it displaces may be more important in a weak opening.

## Develop the engine

Build Ticket Offices in worthwhile cities and stagger Crew visits around each city's independent cooldown. After a festival starts, the Crew can leave and work elsewhere; extra Crews do not stack another show in the same city.

## Convert the advantage

Maintain a developed network of regular festivals while building the actual Tourism, research or diplomatic infrastructure for victory. Flat festival Culture is support for policy development, not an automatic source of Tourism.

## Pros

- Reusable Crews can combine ordinary Worker jobs with festival preparation.
- Several cities can host independent festivals at once.
- Ticket Offices add permanent Culture and an additional festival-time incentive.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Festivals have a short fixed duration and a longer local cooldown.
- Crew travel or parking has an opportunity cost in unfinished improvements.
- Extra Crews cannot stack the same city's festival yields.

## Playing against this civ

- Threaten travelling Crews and force escort or repair duties.
- Pressure cities during their inactive local cooldown.
- Use mobile attacks to disrupt the logistical route linking the next festivals.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving every Crew parked in a city despite unbuilt critical improvements.
- Expecting captured foreign Workers to trigger a show.
- Treating ordinary Culture generation as equivalent to Tourism pressure.
