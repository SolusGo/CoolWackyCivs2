# Indigo Champion - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../TrainerRed/README.md)

**Leader:** Trainer Red  
**Playstyle:** An irreplaceable veteran team and selected tactical tools  
**Suggested victories:** Domination; Science from successful high-value combat

Train and preserve partners, maintain a diverse Champion Team, and take valuable fights without treating a rank as permission to ignore support. A dead partner's Training and history are permanently lost.

## Opening priorities

Use ordinary support troops and safe camps to develop the first veterans. Establish food, Production and the infrastructure needed to replace conventional losses. Select useful partners instead of filling every role with the same unit class.

## Develop the engine

Manage the roster, item cooldowns and Ace support. Check the highest rank's actual effects: ranks replace earlier ranks rather than adding every listed benefit. Use strongest-fielded-type rewards only when the battle is genuinely favourable.

## Convert the advantage

Prepare Mt. Silver around its real eligibility, withdrawal and return constraints. Keep a conventional defense while partners are unavailable, preserve legal return locations, and use the team as part of a complete army.

## Pros

- Training and team support can develop unusually valuable surviving units.
- Successful fights against an eligible strongest fielded type reward more than ordinary casualties.
- Roster tools allow deliberate tactical roles and recovery planning.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Death permanently removes individual Training and veteran history.
- Team capacity and several active tools have gates or cooldowns.
- A temporary withdrawal or a badly composed team can leave conventional cities exposed.

## Playing against this civ

- Focus a developed partner to completion rather than donating repeated survivable exchanges.
- Deny easy strongest-type kills and isolate the team from its supporting units.
- Apply pressure when the main partners are withdrawn or key abilities are cooling down.

## Common mistakes

- Adding the effects of every Training rank together.
- Treating a replacement unit as if it inherits a dead partner's Training.
- Starting a withdrawal without checking the capital defense and later legal return space.
