# Children of the Kingdom - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../TheChildrenOfTheKingdom/README.md)

**Leader:** The Faithful  
**Playstyle:** A fixed prayer cycle with permanent capital development  
**Suggested victories:** Culture or Diplomacy; Science through a protected economy

Pray consistently, build permanent Grace through completed cycles, and protect the capital that receives its lasting yields. Temporary blessings are powerful at the right time, but the petition order cannot be freely rearranged.

## Opening priorities

Found Sanctuary, use the first petition when available, and establish ordinary food, production and defense. Plan Houses of Prayer and a stable home front instead of assuming the prayer cycle provides an instant answer to every threat.

## Develop the engine

Check the next petition and cooldown before scheduling construction or a war. Seek applies empire Culture, Ask supports the capital, and Protection has its own defensive window. Completing the cycle is the route to permanent Grace.

## Convert the advantage

Use developed cities and accumulated capital yields for the chosen victory. Preserve Keepers and coordinate their support with ordinary units. At the Grace cap, continue using the cycle for its actual remaining rewards rather than expecting infinite permanent stacks.

## Pros

- A completed prayer cycle develops permanent capital Faith and Culture.
- The sequence includes economic, Happiness and defensive tools.
- Houses and veteran Keepers fit a stable, protected empire.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Petitions follow a fixed sequence and a speed-scaled cooldown.
- Permanent Grace has a cap and is concentrated in the current capital.
- The player must return to the world map and make valid prayer choices.

## Playing against this civ

- Observe the defensive blessing and avoid attacking a prepared city at its strongest moment.
- Apply pressure when the next petition is an economic effect rather than immediate Protection.
- Contest the capital's productive base and the diplomatic or cultural plan it supports.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting for a perfect moment indefinitely and delaying every later petition.
- Expecting to choose Protection directly from any stage.
- Assuming more completed cycles raise Grace without a cap.
