# Laureate Commonwealth - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../TheLaureateCommonwealth/README.md)

**Leader:** Laurea  
**Playstyle:** Achievement-fed Golden Ages and cultured military finishing  
**Suggested victories:** Culture; Diplomacy or supported Domination

Develop several genuine achievement sources, contribute Hall income, and let the normal Golden Age system do its work. Use the resulting Great Person and combat-experience window to create the next meaningful achievements.

## Opening priorities

Build a stable economy, feasible Wonders and useful cultural infrastructure. Preserve developing military units instead of forcing a Level 3 reward through a bad fight. Ordinary defenses and strategic resources still matter.

## Develop the engine

Use Halls and specialist cities to support a recurring Golden Age plan. Laureate recognizes a broader range of newly created Great People than the Paradise Dreams system; captured or administrative replacements are not equivalent new achievements.

## Convert the advantage

Convert Golden Age support into real Tourism, diplomacy or trained armies. Let ranged damage create useful Crowning Blow targets, preserve upgraded Champions, and keep functioning Halls out of avoidable resistance or loss.

## Pros

- World Wonders, new Great People and developing military veterans contribute to one native meter.
- Golden Ages support specialist Great Person generation and military combat XP.
- Champions can finish wounded military targets and heal after qualifying kills.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Points do not create an immediate custom Golden Age or change its native threshold.
- Veteran recognition is once per lifetime and does not reward imported high-level history.
- The Champion's attack bonus is not a defensive, healthy-target or city-attack bonus.

## Playing against this civ

- Avoid feeding wounded military units to a preserved Champion.
- Contest realistic Wonders and threaten the cities supporting specialists and functioning Halls.
- Apply pressure between Golden Ages rather than assuming the experience modifier lasts permanently.

## Common mistakes

- Counting captured or National Wonders as the World Wonder achievement.
- Expecting repeated Level 3 recognition from the same veteran.
- Treating Golden Age Points as Tourism or an automatic immediate Golden Age start.
