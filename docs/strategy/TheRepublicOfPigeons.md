# Pigeon Republic - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../TheRepublicOfPigeons/README.md)

**Leader:** Postmaster General Coo  
**Playstyle:** Protected land trade and distributed origin-city yields  
**Suggested victories:** Science or Culture; Diplomacy with a strong trade economy

Establish safe outgoing caravans, then decide whether to concentrate route yields or activate several Roost cities. The bonus belongs to the origin of a qualifying land route, and domestic routes are fully useful.

## Opening priorities

Use the Carrier Pigeon's sight to find safe routes and defensible destinations. Establish normal food and production first; protect a caravan before making the economy depend on its recurring Science and Culture.

## Develop the engine

Place Roosts in real land-route origins. Concentrating routes can support a strong research city; distributing them can activate several separate Production bonuses. Check that each route remains active after a war or access change.

## Convert the advantage

Maintain land-route capacity and the cities that multiply its output. The Renaissance increases the recurring route yields, but maritime routes and incoming trade never become substitutes for qualifying caravans.

## Pros

- Domestic food or Production trade can also provide the signature yields.
- Several outgoing land routes stack Science and Culture at the origin.
- Roosts add useful Culture and an active-route Production incentive.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Plundered, interrupted and missing routes remove the dependent output.
- Sea and incoming routes do not qualify.
- A Roost's conditional Production is a single city bonus, not a bonus per route.

## Playing against this civ

- Patrol or raid caravan corridors and threaten their actual origins.
- Force unsafe routes or unfavourable destination choices through military pressure.
- Use maritime advantages on a map where reliable land trading is constrained.

## Common mistakes

- Counting sea routes or trade arriving in the city.
- Multiplying the Roost's Production once for every caravan.
- Stacking the entire economy in an origin that cannot be defended.
