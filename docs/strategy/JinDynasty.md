# Jin - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../JinDynasty/README.md)

**Leader:** Wanyan Aguda  
**Playstyle:** Resource-backed cavalry, selected landmarks and tributary logistics  
**Suggested victories:** Domination or Science

Build the Landmark and Grassland infrastructure that supports a cavalry army, while balancing stored Horses against paid recruitment. A large horse inventory is a strategic asset; spending it changes the maximum-HP support available to the army.

## Opening priorities

Use Mounted Villagers and the capital's starting development opportunity to build food and production. Choose Landmark paths with a longer-term plan and remember that effects follow completed native construction, not a selection click.

## Develop the engine

Protect the chosen Grassland sites and place useful War Stables within their real range. Compare recruitment's Horse and Gold cost with the HP benefit of retaining the stockpile. Use Emissaries and Tributaries when their access and diplomatic conditions are available.

## Convert the advantage

Preserve the experienced cavalry force, protect its resource locations and keep recruitment financially sustainable. Use Bribe only with a safe follow-up plan: replacements are Barbarian-controlled threats to both armies, not free Jin soldiers.

## Pros

- Mounted Villagers combine Worker utility with strong development mobility.
- Stored Horses support eligible cavalry maximum HP and enable recruitment choices.
- Landmarks and Tributaries provide several connected economic and military plans.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Horses, Gold, legal city deployment and prerequisites all constrain recruitment.
- Pillage can stop a Grassland site's generation and HP contribution.
- A selected Landmark is a permanent choice, and the system has several management dependencies.

## Playing against this civ

- Threaten active Grasslands and War Stable approaches.
- Force the owner to spend reserves before the main cavalry campaign.
- Use appropriate anti-mounted forces and avoid leaving a valuable cluster vulnerable to an Emissary.

## Common mistakes

- Treating a Landmark selection as a completed building.
- Expecting stored Horses to buff every military class or tanks.
- Assuming Bribe delivers friendly units, or that HP-cap adjustments create free repeated healing.
