# Saba - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../KingdomOfSaba/README.md)

**Leader:** Karib'il Watar  
**Playstyle:** Low-management River settlement and practical early defense  
**Suggested victories:** Science or Culture through population and conventional infrastructure

Choose productive River sites and take the Desert city-centre bonus where the surrounding land can still feed the city. The ability rewards a good settlement, not every city placed beside a visible patch of sand.

## Opening priorities

Scout rivers and the actual city-centre terrain. Prioritize enough food, production and resources over a nominal Desert bonus. Wadi Sentinels help watch and defend suitable dry approaches.

## Develop the engine

Build Cisterns where the active Water Mill requirements permit them. Turn the added food and Gold into citizens, research infrastructure and a workable treasury. Further expansion should be justified by the full site quality.

## Convert the advantage

Use the developed population and ordinary science or cultural programme to finish the game. Upgrade defenses when needed; the Sentinel's specific Desert-defense and Sight additions do not continue through its upgrade.

## Pros

- Useful settlement bonuses operate automatically without a custom action cycle.
- River and Desert-centre conditions can stack.
- Cisterns improve an ordinary food-and-Gold development plan.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- The signature yields depend on the actual city centre and River status.
- There is no direct Science or Tourism bonus.
- The Sentinel's unique bonuses are temporary to that unit type and are not an attacking boost.

## Playing against this civ

- Contest the strongest River sites and productive desert approaches.
- Fight away from the Sentinel's preferred defending terrain.
- Pressure a weak-food settlement whose owner chased the Desert label at the expense of development.

## Common mistakes

- Counting adjacent Desert or an Oasis as the required city-centre/River condition.
- Assuming a Cistern ignores normal Water Mill prerequisites.
- Expecting the unique defensive promotion to make a strong offensive army or survive upgrading.
