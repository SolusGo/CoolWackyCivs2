# The Boundless City - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../TheBoundlessCity/README.md)

**Leader:** The First Architect  
**Playstyle:** One expanding municipality with a finite workforce  
**Suggested victories:** Science or Diplomacy; Domination through the special siege/subjugation rules

Grow one productive capital, expand connected territory, and assign real citizens to worthwhile jobs. The city can own a great deal of land, but ownership is not a substitute for workers, food or the engine's workforce limits.

## Opening priorities

Choose strong capital food, production and defensible access. Improve the best nearby sites and establish the Registry. Let growth and culture expansion support one another instead of reserving every distant job immediately.

## Develop the engine

Review the Workforce's actual food balance, assigned citizens and remote-job value. Build the infrastructure and Surveyors needed for the selected frontier. Prioritize jobs that improve the whole city rather than a distant tile's isolated yield.

## Convert the advantage

Support a conventional research or diplomatic plan with a mature capital. If seeking Domination, learn the reclaim, legal demolition and protected-capital subjugation rules; ordinary occupation and unlimited extra cities are not the plan.

## Pros

- A large connected territory can offer a broad menu of jobs and resources.
- Registry and Surveyor development reinforce frontier growth.
- Capital-loss survival and special siege interactions provide alternatives to ordinary occupation.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- The capital and its supporting workforce are highly concentrated strategic assets.
- Remote jobs still consume real population and obey implemented capacity limits.
- Foreign-city acquisition and Domination use special rules rather than a normal expanding city empire.

## Playing against this civ

- Contest important land before the contiguous border reaches it.
- Raid productive approaches and threaten food or key assigned improvements.
- Pressure the capital economy instead of assuming empty owned territory supplies unlimited output.

## Common mistakes

- Counting every owned tile as an automatically worked tile.
- Expanding reservations without supporting food and population.
- Expecting ordinary city capture to produce a permanent second municipal centre.

## Who pressures whom

Named custom-civilization matchups are conditional mechanical analysis, not tested win rates. All custom civs are human-only and multiplayer is unsupported; these comparisons are for theorycraft or explicitly configured scenarios, not ordinary random AI opponents. General counterplay applies to normal single-player games.

| Situation | Other civ | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opportunity | [McPvP](McPvP.md) | A closely watched single-city approach limits the number of distinct city objectives available in a match cycle; it does not prevent an objective from succeeding. |
| Opportunity | [The Conical Union](TheConicalUnion.md) | A one-city plan does not depend on keeping several non-capital connection bonuses active, which helps in a map dominated by chokepoints and broken routes. |
| Threat | [Luna Network](https://github.com/SolusGo/CoolWackyCivs/blob/main/docs/strategy/LunaNetwork.md) | Fast settlement can claim useful land and constrain future contiguous expansion before it arrives. |
| Threat | [The Filthy Realm](https://github.com/SolusGo/CoolWackyCivs/blob/main/docs/strategy/FilthyRealm.md) | A city-targeted debuff affects a large portion of the municipality's total output. |

## Implementation limits

Remote work remains subject to the documented native capacity and settlement boundaries. Use the Workforce and the engine-limit report as the authority for the currently supported jobs.

## Rules and evidence

Advice follows the shipped mechanics. Map, era, army composition, difficulty and execution can reverse a matchup. Culture supports policies; a Culture victory still needs Tourism and its normal conditions. Native inherited unit/building statistics follow the active CP ruleset.
