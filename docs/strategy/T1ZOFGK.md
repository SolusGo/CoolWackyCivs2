# T1 - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../T1ZOFGK/README.md)

**Leader:** Faker  
**Playstyle:** Five persistent roles and long-lived upgrade development  
**Suggested victories:** Domination; Culture supported by Headquarters development

Train and preserve the original roster, keep useful role combinations close enough to work, and maintain the Gold and technology for genuine upgrades. A successor restores a role after a delay, not the veteran history that was lost.

## Opening priorities

Develop the first locally created Recruits with enough conventional support to survive. Establish Headquarters and a viable economy instead of sending all five valuable roles into an unsupported opening attack.

## Develop the engine

Assign tasks deliberately: Zeus pressures cities, Oner scouts routes, Faker supports contact, Gumayusi finishes wounded enemies and Keria supports legal healing. Keep enough roles in range when the formation matters and save Gold for real unit-type upgrades.

## Convert the advantage

Convert accumulated development into a technologically current combined-arms army. Preserve roles and experience through retreats. If a member is lost, defend through the vacancy timer and normally produce an eligible replacement at Headquarters.

## Pros

- Genuine repeated upgrades build persistent development without the small tier cap used by some other veterans.
- Distinct roles and nearby-roster formations support a coherent core army.
- Headquarters help train an appropriate supporting force.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- A death loses that unit's XP and upgrade history.
- Formation strength depends on nearby distinct living roles, not the count of ordinary troops.
- Successors require a timer and eligible normal production; they do not appear for free.

## Playing against this civ

- Focus a valuable role to completion and exploit the resulting formation and replacement gap.
- Separate the roster or attack where the concentrated core is absent.
- Pressure upgrade Gold and technology rather than letting the same veterans develop indefinitely.

## Common mistakes

- Counting ordinary troops or duplicate role labels toward Five as One.
- Expecting purchases or gifts to fill an eligible successor vacancy.
- Confusing promotions or administrative conversions with genuine unit-type upgrades.
