# Substitute Classroom - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../TheSubstituteClassroom/README.md)

**Leader:** Mr. Garvey  
**Playstyle:** Timed economic choices with a risky combat surge  
**Suggested victories:** Domination or Science; Culture through selected windows

Choose the Roll Call effect that supports the coming eight turns, and treat Insubordination as a real risk rather than a controllable countdown. Detention can be powerful, but arriving unprepared for it wastes the opportunity.

## Opening priorities

Establish an ordinary economy and a defensible army while waiting for the first Roll Call. Decide whether the first priority is research, infrastructure or military readiness before choosing a student.

## Develop the engine

Use targeted choices for an actual plan. Timothy trades the larger specialist modifier for broad output without correction risk. With other students, retain the Happiness and army readiness to handle a third correction and Detention.

## Convert the advantage

Coordinate a prepared force with any combat window and keep the economy useful between choices. Principal's Offices help the empire-wide Detention Happiness cost, but building more Offices does not repeatedly reduce that penalty.

## Pros

- Roll Call can focus temporary benefits on several different strategic needs.
- Detention rewards a prepared military force and qualifying enemy kills.
- Hall Monitors combine territorial combat with a local kill-Culture role.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Choices have fixed cooldowns and do not remain active permanently.
- Most named students introduce correction risk; the exact third-correction timing is not guaranteed.
- Detention costs Happiness and does not substitute for an army already in position.

## Playing against this civ

- Avoid a decisive battle during a prepared Detention combat window.
- Pressure the empire during inactive intervals and force a less convenient student choice.
- Do not feed easy friendly-territory kills to Hall Monitors.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming the correction outcome or Detention start can be scheduled exactly.
- Choosing a military buff while the army is still travelling.
- Expecting multiple Offices to reduce the global Happiness penalty multiple times.

## Who pressures whom

All custom civs are human-only and multiplayer is unsupported; these comparisons are for theorycraft or explicitly configured scenarios, not ordinary random AI opponents. General counterplay applies to normal single-player games.
