# The Substitute Classroom — Mr. Garvey

From **Room 101**, turn attendance into authority. A human-only **economy / military timing** civilization for BNW + Community Patch v151, complexity **3/5**.

- **Insubordinate and Churlish:** every 12 player turns, choose an 8-turn student bonus: military Production, Culture, Science, combat, or Timothy's reliable broad economy. The first Roll Call unlocks 12 turns after founding the capital; pending choices wait indefinitely.
- **Patience / DETENTION:** the first four students have a 50% correction chance. The third correction resets Insubordination and triggers five turns of +20% military strength/Production and Culture from kills, with a temporary -2 Global Happiness. Timothy never corrects you.
- **Hall Monitor / Pikeman:** +15% strength and Culture from kills in friendly territory; +10% additional strength during Detention. Normal Pikeman role/path/model; Hall-specific training ends on upgrade.
- **Principal's Office / Public School:** all ordinary school benefits plus +2 Culture/+2 Production, +10% local Science during Detention, and empire-wide non-stacking relief reducing its penalty to -1.

Open the **ROLL CALL** launcher for the blackboard attendance sheet, cooldown, all five choices, current bonus, patience and Detention status. Choose a specialist bonus for a planned push, or Timothy for a reliable economy. Time the third correction for war and protect your Offices. The main limits are short windows, a four-turn gap after a prompt selection, and correction uncertainty; turn counts stay fixed on every speed.

Original 2D leader art, distinct unit/flag/building/emblem graphics and English dialogue are included. Automated validation is separate from **NOT TESTED IN GAME** native rendering, yields, combat and saves. Detention also rewards attributed lethal nuclear/collateral damage; captures, disbands and friendly fire do not pay.
