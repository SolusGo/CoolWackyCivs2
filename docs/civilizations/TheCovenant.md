# The Covenant

**The Scribe — Written on the Heart.**

Earn your next turn by recalling five player-selected NIV passages. Timed study gives way to reference recall and exact words in numbered sections. Errors restart the passage, studied attempts earn no memory reward, and recurring reviews culminate in all-five exams every ten campaign turns.

Written on the Heart provides +1 Culture per city and once-per-turn Faith/Culture for an unaided recall. Scriptoria replace Shrines with 2 Faith and 1 Culture; Tabernacles replace Temples with 3 Faith and 1 Science. The civ uses stock Egyptian and Ramesses artwork.

Prefer protected religious/cultural development. The recall gate is the main personal challenge; ordinary settlement, research and defense still matter. The system enforces an in-game practice gate, not proof against outside answers or edited files. Native rendering, keyboard dispatch and save acceptance remain unverified.
