# The Covenant

**The Scribe — Written on the Heart.**

Recall five player-selected NIV passages, or skip the check for the current turn without a reward. Timed study gives way to reference recall and exact words in numbered sections. Errors restart the passage, studied attempts earn no memory reward, and recurring reviews culminate in all-five exams every ten campaign turns.

Written on the Heart provides +1 Culture per city and once-per-turn Faith/Culture for an unaided recall. Scriptoria replace Shrines with 2 Faith and 1 Culture; Tabernacles replace Temples with 3 Faith and 1 Science. Original navy-and-gold artwork depicts the Scribe, a sandstone scriptorium and a linen tabernacle, with a Scripture/quill emblem and matching recall interface.

Prefer protected religious/cultural development. The recall gate is the main personal challenge; ordinary settlement, research and defense still matter. The practice gate can be dismissed with Skip this turn; skipping retains completed progress and earns no mastery or reward. Native rendering, keyboard dispatch and save acceptance remain unverified.
