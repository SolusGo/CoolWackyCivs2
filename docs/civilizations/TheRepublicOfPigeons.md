# The Republic of Pigeons

**Postmaster General Coo** leads a trade and exploration civilization from **Coopolis**. Reliable correspondence supplies knowledge and culture; busy rooftops supply practical work.

| Feature | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| Messages on the Wing | +2 Science/+2 Culture per outgoing land route in its origin city; +3 each from Renaissance onward. Domestic/international qualify. Sea/incoming do not. |
| Carrier Pigeon | Scout replacement with +1 Sight, existing ignore-terrain movement and unchanged recon/ruin/upgrade behavior. |
| Rooftop Roost | Full Caravansary replacement plus +1 Culture and +2 Production while at least one outgoing land route is active. Production never stacks past +2. |

Scout safe routes, build Roosts and protect caravans. Internal routes let the republic feed growing cities while still advancing research and Social Policies. Concentrated routes favor a strong science origin; distributing them activates Production in several cities. Maritime empires gain less from the UA, and hostile neighbors can interrupt the network. Coo prefers friendly trade, exploration and defensible borders, with Science, Culture and Diplomacy priorities.

The unit uses the standard Scout model. Original portraits, loading art, postal icons, flags and a map are included. Native UI, game-start and save tests are still required; [validation record](../../TheRepublicOfPigeons/docs/Testing.md).
