# The 11 Cats — Austin, King of the House

A peaceful, human-only civilization for BNW and Community Patch v151. Austin was adopted in 2013; across the eras, ten unique companions join his household. The supplied emblem shows the white, long-haired King at its center.

**King of the House:** genuine We Love the King Day celebrations last 25% longer. House Cats Settle In for +1 Culture and exact personal local bonuses, with three residents per city. Specific family combinations grant modest local bonds. All identities survive displacement through protective recovery.

**House Cat:** untrainable, maintenance-free, 2-Movement civilian companions created by chapters. Settle through The Household, or rehome with a speed-scaled cooldown. Austin never spawns as an additional unit.

**Cat Tree:** Garden replacement with +25% GP generation and +1 Culture, no Fresh Water requirement, and +1 Food once while the city has residents.

Develop four to six cities, plan home combinations and pursue Culture or Diplomacy. There are no direct Science or offensive military bonuses. Charlotte and Sisi celebrate safety and affection; suffering and displacement generate no rewards. The final milestone is **11 / 11 — Everyone Is Home.**
