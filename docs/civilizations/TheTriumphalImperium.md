# The Triumphal Imperium

**Imperator Aurelion — One Victory Demands Another.**

Build a sustainable army before the first war, then turn earned victories into
military strength and replacement troops. Momentum starts at 0, caps at 6, and
fades by 1 for each complete owner-turn cycle without a qualifying victory.
Major military combat kills give +1; first conquest of each major city site gives
+2. Defensive victories between turns count. City-States, barbarians and civilians do not.

Each Momentum gives +2% military Strength and +4% military Production. At 4+,
original **Unbroken** gain +1 maximum Movement and **Hall of Triumphs** cities
gain +10% local military Production. Unbroken replace Swordsmen and retain their
base balance, adding a permanent +10% attack promotion. Halls replace Barracks,
add +1 Culture, and are granted in major conquests once Bronze Working is known.

The upper-right six-laurel panel shows current Momentum and percentages. Hover
for both conditional statuses. Preserve units, secure Iron and use ranged support;
an unsuccessful campaign can strip both threshold advantages. Counterplay denies
easy kills and forces pauses. Domination is preferred; economy and research stay normal.

The anti-farming rule is once-per-site conquest Momentum, including after
razing/refounding. Movement activation never refills current moves. Native turn
settlement precedes the decay boundary. Static/source/database and Lua-mock
validation are performed separately from still-pending native play/render/save tests.
