# The Children of the Kingdom

**Leader:** The Faithful · **Capital:** Sanctuary · **Human-playable only**

Inspired respectfully by the Lord's Prayer, this fictional civilization pursues Culture and Diplomacy through steady devotion and defensive perseverance. Its early bonuses are modest; permanent Grace grows only through full prayer cycles, independently of conquest.

**Thy Kingdom Come:** Open Prayer at the upper right and activate seven petitions in order, once every five Standard turns, scaled to the active game speed. Address nourishes the capital; Opening Glory grants Faith; Seek improves actual city Culture; Ask provides daily bread; Repent gives empire Happiness; Protection strengthens cities and friendly-territory defenders; Closing Glory completes the cycle for Grace, Golden Age Points and Faith from Houses of Prayer.

Each of at most seven Grace adds +1 Faith/+1 Culture per turn in the capital. Further cycles replace the new stack with extra Golden Age Points. The Prayer panel shows every current effect, cooldown, stage, Grace and Production reserve. Saving/reloading preserves progress.

**Keeper of the Faith:** Pikeman replacement with native stats/role. Human Kingdom Keepers retain Stand Firm through upgrades: +15% friendly-territory defensive strength and +5 HP on eligible friendly healing turns. Protection is a separate +15% defense. Neither improves attacks.

**House of Prayer:** Temple replacement retaining active native behavior, plus +1 Faith/+1 Culture for human Kingdom owners. Every completed owned House grants 10 Faith when Closing Glory is prayed.

Build infrastructure, protect the capital and keep the prayer rhythm. Losing a city does not erase Grace, but it can reduce House rewards. ASK preserves excess Production in a visible saved reserve until a finite capital queue can use it. CP's +20% native city-strength effect also affects ranged city strength; unit effects remain defensive. AI assignment is disabled, with no automated AI prayer system. Native in-game verification remains pending.
