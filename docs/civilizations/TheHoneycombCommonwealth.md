# The Honeycomb Commonwealth

**Speaker Mellia**, elected by the Council of Hives, leads an intelligent honeybee federation from **First Comb**. Human-only; BNW + Community Patch v151. Science / Culture; complexity 3/5.

| Feature | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| A Thousand Small Hands | +1 city Production per qualifying worked Plantation, capped at +4; at least two grant +1 Culture |
| Forager → Scout | Active Scout +1 Movement and +1 Sight; normal combat, requirements and upgrades; unique Sight lost on upgrade |
| Honey Exchange → Market | All Market effects, +1 Food and +1 additional Gold per two qualifying worked Plantations, capped at +2 |

Plantations must be built, unpillaged, owned and actually worked by that city within three tiles. Unworked plantations give nothing. Four worked Plantations plus an Exchange adds +4 Production, +1 Culture, +1 Food and +2 Gold beyond ordinary effects. Caps never scale.

**Timing:** the reliable CP fallback snapshots at owner end and stages the next payout; interturn changes wait one further boundary. Midturn changes and reloads preserve existing snapshots. Exchange loss clears Gold and city transfers clear HCC dummies. Literal pre-economic owner-start sampling could not be guaranteed without DLL changes; in-game help documents this.

Explore early, improve resources with ordinary Workers, balance assignments against Food and protect agriculture. Modest bonuses support growth, policies and research; military strength remains ordinary. Amber bee artwork includes a static diplomatic scene and the Scout's native 3D model.
