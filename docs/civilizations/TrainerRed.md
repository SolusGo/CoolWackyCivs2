# The Indigo Champion

**Leader:** Trainer Red, the Silent Champion. **Capital:** Pallet Town. **Ability:** The Living Legend. Preferred victories: Domination, then Science.

From a young trainer's first steps to the silent final challenge on Mt. Silver, Red develops a small group of irreplaceable military partners. Battle-earned Training unlocks exclusive ranks. The Champion Team grows from two to six, learns from worthy opponents and collects Ribbons through first original-capital conquests.

| Unique | Replaces | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pokémon Ace | Great General | Native General while detached; attach for strength, Training, healing and Battle Item |
| Pokémon Center | Hospital | Recovery, Food, veteran instruction, upgrade refunds and constrained airlift |
| Indigo Plateau | Heroic Epic | Morale, military Production, six slots, team-rank yields and a persistent Hall of Fame |
| Journey to Mt. Silver | Civilization-specific project | Modern-era withdrawal, temporary economic penalties, then upgraded Summit-Trained partners with six distinct identities |

Protect veterans early, build a balanced team in the middle eras and prepare a safe empire before withdrawing to Mt. Silver. Legendary partners are exceptionally strong together; lost experience cannot be purchased back. Air power, strategic overstretch and losing key partners remain serious vulnerabilities.
