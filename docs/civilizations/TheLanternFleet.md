# The Lantern Fleet — High Keeper Maren

A defensive naval economy favoring Science and Culture. Capital: First Beacon. Human-selectable only; scenario AI uses the same rules.

- **No Harbor Goes Dark:** +2 Science/+1 Culture per coastal city with at least one owned naval combat ship inside its center at owner-turn start.
- **Beacon Cutter:** active Trireme replacement; +1 Sight/+15% friendly-territory Strength on attack and defense through its normally retained unique promotion.
- **Signal Lighthouse:** active Lighthouse effects/+1 permanent Culture; +2 conditional Gold with a qualifying guard at the snapshot.

One ship is enough; no stacking or era scaling. Adjacent, foreign, civilian and embarked units never qualify. Movement after the snapshot does not change that turn's state. Guarded Lighthouse total: +2 Science/+2 Culture/+2 Gold plus normal Lighthouse effects.

Build around safe coastal settlements, shelter your ships before turn start, and use retained snapshots to send them out afterward. Inland settlements and unguarded harbors lose the conditional economy. Capture clears both conditional buildings immediately.
