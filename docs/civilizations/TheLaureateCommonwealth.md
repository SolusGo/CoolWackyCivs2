# The Laureate Commonwealth

**Leader:** Laurea, Keeper of the Unfading Crown. **Capital:** Daphne. Human-only, BNW + Community Patch v151.

**The Evergreen Crown** recognizes World Wonders, Great People and Level 3 military veterans with normal Golden Age Points. Rewards scale by current Era and game speed. Golden Ages add 15% city Great Person generation and 15% combat XP.

**Laureate Champions** retain all ordinary Swordsman requirements and stats. Their lasting Crowning Blow promotion adds 15% attacking Strength against wounded units and 15 HP healing on military kills. **Halls of Laurels** retain the Amphitheater's normal effects and Writing slot, adding one Local Happiness and one Golden Age Point per functioning Hall per owner turn.

Build Halls, create cultural Great People, finish meaningful Wonders and preserve experienced infantry. Culture is the favored victory; careful military finishing and a prosperous civic economy support Domination and Diplomacy. The Commonwealth receives no additional base Strength, Movement, Tourism, free units, free Great People or Golden Age duration, so expansion and technology remain important.

Golden Age awards enter the ordinary meter; the engine starts the Golden Age on its next normal check. Great Person rewards wait for native creation/conversion to settle. Native rendering, gameplay and real save/load acceptance remain pending.
