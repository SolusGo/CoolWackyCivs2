# The Urgal

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Leader | Urgal |
| Capital | The Feast |
| Origin | MCPVP Hardcore Games |
| Preferred victory | Domination |
| Ability | Strength II: one initial potion and one per new Era; stockpile without a cap, consume for +50% melee attack against units |
| Unit | Urgal Rusher (Swordsman): 14 Strength, 2 Movement, 80 Production, no Iron; permanent Pot Chaser heals 15 HP per kill during Strength II |
| Building | Brewing Stand (Barracks): 75 Production, 1 maintenance, inherited training XP and +10% eligible melee Production; never creates potions |
| Playability | Human and AI; Windows single-player with BNW and CP v151 |

Prepare the army, position it and then use the upper-right **DRINK STRENGTH II** button. The current eligible army receives the effect; new recruits do not. Duration is 3/5/7/12 turns on Quick/Standard/Epic/Marathon, followed by two turns of Aftertaste before another bottle may be used. Defense, city attacks, ranged attacks, ships and aircraft gain nothing. A normal upgrade preserves an eligible participant's remaining window; ownership transfers end its participation.

Urgal is average between bursts and exceptionally dangerous while pressing a well-timed attack. Scarce potions, mobile enemies, terrain and cheap delaying screens make wasted turns costly. There is no direct economic or scientific boost. The AI activates only around concentrated ready armies with immediate legal contact and visible enemies, an enemy city or an invasion.

The implementation preview includes a static illustrated leader, ordinary Swordsman model and English text. Automated SQL/Lua/source/package checks are distinct from native gameplay acceptance, which remains pending.
