# The Kingdom of the Unrung Bell

**Keeper Sable · Capital: Stillhaven · Culture / Science · Advanced · human-only**

The Kingdom remembers a bell whose terrible sound ended an age of war. Sable protects the peace that grew around its silence, while the Bellwardens guard the chains.

| Unique | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Silence Before the Storm | Every 5 completed peaceful owner-turns earn one Quietude, maximum 3. Each gives every city +1 Culture. Major/city-state war consumes it for the Great Toll. Barbarians do not interrupt it. |
| The Great Toll | +5%/+10%/+15% friendly-land combat to land military units according to consumed stacks, immediate activation plus five full owner-turn windows. Additional wars never refresh it; complete peace removes it. |
| Bellwarden (Pikeman) | All active Pikeman rules, with permanent +1 Sight and +15% friendly-land combat retained on upgrades. Additive +30% with Toll III. Native Pikeman models/flags retained. |
| Silent Belfry (Temple) | All active Temple benefits and requirements, +1 permanent Culture, +1 Science per Quietude stack. At cap, +4 Culture/+3 Science total unique additions per Belfry city. |

Complete peaceful turns, expand to spread Culture, and establish Belfries to support research. When war comes, defend through the temporary surge, then seek complete peace to rebuild the economy. Expanding too aggressively can leave a wide empire exposed after the Toll expires. This civilization has no unconditional Science, Tourism or worldwide combat increase.

**Preview limits:** native gameplay and saves still need testing. CP friendly territory includes open borders, and ranged units check their firing tile; the requested exclusively owned target rule is not implemented exactly.
