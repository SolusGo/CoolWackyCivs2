# T1 - The ZOFGK Dynasty

| Detail | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Leader / Capital | Faker / T1 HQ |
| Ability | ZOFGK: five permanent roster roles and exclusive formations |
| Unique unit | T1 Recruit, Warrior replacement with combat XP and genuine-upgrade development |
| Unique building | T1 Headquarters, Barracks replacement with Culture, local melee Production and successor recruitment |
| Victory / Complexity | Domination, Culture / 4 of 5 |
| AI support | Human playable only |

Build the first five Recruits, including the starting replacement, into Zeus, Oner, Faker, Gumayusi and Keria. Preserve them across the eras: each genuine Recruit upgrade adds +4% permanent Strength. Coordinate three/four roles within two hexes for +10% Strength or all five for +15%. A lost veteran's role can return through Headquarters recruitment after a speed-scaled cooldown, but its XP and development are gone.

The army grows through training and careful preservation. No free replacements, global yields or base-strength edits are used. Scattered troops, veteran deaths and weak technology can break the dynasty. The right-side roster panel explains current units, bonuses and replacement timing.

This tribute depicts the historical 2022-2024 lineup, the 2022 Worlds final appearance and the consecutive 2023/2024 victories. Assets include the official T1 red mark and original painted leader/roster/unit/building illustrations.
