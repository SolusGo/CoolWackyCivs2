# The Kingdom of Saba — Karib'il Watar

From **Ma'rib**, an ancient South Arabian kingdom turns seasonal water into enduring settlements. A simple historical civilization for **Science / Culture**, complexity **2/5**, human-only under BNW + Community Patch v151.

- **Where the Desert Blooms:** River city centers receive +1 Food/+1 Production. Desert centers, including Desert Hills, receive +1 Culture. Flat city bonuses stack while Saba owns a qualifying city, including an acquired city.
- **Wadi Sentinel (Spearman):** inherits the active Spearman, plus +15% defending strength on underlying Desert and +1 Sight. Both unique bonuses expire on upgrade; attacking receives no unique strength bonus.
- **Ma'rib Cistern (Water Mill):** inherits all active Water Mill benefits and requirements, then adds +1 Food/+1 Gold.

Seek River sites, develop growth infrastructure and protect dry approaches. A Desert River city with a Cistern adds +2 Food/+1 Production/+1 Culture/+1 Gold beyond an ordinary Water Mill. Desert settlements still need workable land; the ability grants no irrigation spreading, plot yields, direct Science or Tourism. No custom panel is needed.

Original 2D painting/symbols, native Spearman model and English localization are included. Automated SQL/source/Lua/package checks are distinct from **NOT TESTED IN GAME** rendering, yields, combat and saves. Start new games for Saba; existing identifiers are retained but native older-save continuation is unverified.
