# The Twelfth Hour

**Leader:** The Keeper of Seconds. **Capital:** Last Minute. **Human selectable only.**

A mysterious industrial society measures its achievements against the Grand Clock. Every factory and observatory follows a shared schedule, culminating in the Grand Chime.

| Unique | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| When the Clock Strikes | +75% Production and +50% Science in all owned cities on played turns 6, 12, 18… |
| Pendulum Guard | Pikeman with +1 Movement and +15% strength in friendly territory, retained through upgrades. |
| Municipal Clocktower | Workshop with all active CP benefits, +1 permanent Culture, and +2 flat Production during Chime. |

Develop several productive research cities, prepare important builds for the next sixth-turn harvest, and reinforce threatened territory with Pendulum Guards. Favor Science or industry-supported Domination, while Clocktower Culture and industrial output preserve other options. Normal turns carry no penalty; weaker infrastructure still limits the value of percentage bonuses.

The first five played turns are normal at every speed; cities, capitals, eras and saves do not restart the cycle. One notification marks each Chime. There is no continuously updated countdown or new top panel. Original art and full English localization are included; unit battlefield art reuses the standard Pikeman.
