# The Republic of First Drafts

Editor-in-Chief Marginalia leads a scholarly republic whose Editorial Department regards every institution as a work in progress. First Edition is its capital. Intermediate complexity (3/5); preferred victories are Science and Culture. Human-only; requires BNW + Community Patch v151.

**Nothing Is Ever Final:** the first possession of each qualifying ordinary Building Class grants 12 Culture and 20 Gold on Standard speed, scaled by the active construction percentage. Evaluation happens at the next owner-turn scan while the building is still present. This empire-wide class history is permanent across losses and saves.

**Ink Mortar:** active Catapult rules with +1 Movement and +1 Sight. Extra Sight is lost on upgrade; normal setup, siege attacks and resource requirements apply. It uses the Catapult model.

**Revision Workshop:** active Workshop benefits plus +1 Culture and +1 Science for each four current qualifying city classes, capped at +3 Science. It counts its own Workshop class. Its current-city count is independent of the historical UA ledger and decreases when buildings are lost.

Diversify early institutions to fund policies and development. Build broad research cities to reach the Workshop's 4/8/12 thresholds. Duplicate national classes still contribute locally. The civilization offers positioning advantages for siege units; combat statistics are unchanged.

Wonders, Palace, dummies, hidden controls and nonconstructible specials are excluded. Eligible purchases, free/gifted buildings and captures can count. Silent external removals reconcile by the next owner turn. Existing progressed saves without a ledger seed present classes without retroactive income. Native gameplay/render/save acceptance is pending.
