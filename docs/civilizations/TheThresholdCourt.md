# The Threshold Court

**Queen Elsewhere — Every Door Leads Somewhere.** An ancient kingdom joined by secret doors, human-only in BNW + Community Patch v151. Her throne room has thirteen doors. Twelve connect her realm; the thirteenth has never opened.

| Unique | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| A Door in Every Wall | One paid, unlimited-distance transfer per owner-turn between owned Threshold House city tiles. Eligible land melee military units only; destination military slot must be empty. Origin ready at T+8, arrival exhausted until next owner-turn. Gold: 50 + 25 × native current Era index. |
| Hingeguard | Active CP Pikeman +1 Movement, +15% Combat Strength on friendly territory; preserves other properties, resources and upgrades. |
| Threshold House | Active CP Market +1 Culture and portal endpoint. No further passive economic effects. |

Construct two Threshold Houses, put a Hingeguard on the departure city tile, select **Threshold Transfer**, choose an empty destination and confirm. Cancel costs nothing. The original unit crosses, retaining health and experience. Budget Gold and keep several ready origins; an empty distant arrival city is as important as a strong army. Fight on the following turn. Strategic reinforcement favors Domination and the defense of distant Science centers.

The network cannot send ranged/civilian/naval/embarked units or arrive in foreign cities. It cannot heal or enable an immediate attack. A used origin needs eight turns and all origins share the one-transfer empire allowance. The unit uses native Pikeman field art; Queen Elsewhere uses a static original diplomacy painting. Native runtime/render/save acceptance remains pending.
