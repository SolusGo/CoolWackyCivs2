# The Republic of Pigeons

Led by **Postmaster General Coo**, with **Coopolis** as its capital. A slate blue and cream republic of couriers, rooftop assemblies and dependable correspondence. Requires Brave New World and **Community Patch v151**; packaged in Cool Wacky Civs 2 version 5.

![Postmaster General Coo](../art-source/TheRepublicOfPigeons/CooAndCoopolis.png)

| Unique | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Messages on the Wing | Each active outgoing **land** route grants its origin +2 Science and +2 Culture, increasing to +3 each in the Renaissance. Domestic and international routes qualify and stack. Sea and incoming routes grant nothing. |
| Carrier Pigeon (Scout) | Inherits the active Scout's stats, role, recon promotions, ruins and upgrades. +1 Sight and retained ignore-terrain-cost promotion. Normal embarkation and impassable-terrain rules apply. Uses the standard Scout 3D model with original portrait/flag icons. |
| Rooftop Roost (Caravansary) | Retains all active Caravansary properties and companion data. Adds +1 Culture. One or more outgoing land routes activate **+2 Production total** in that city; zero routes or removing the Roost removes that bonus. |

These are base building yields: ordinary city yield modifiers, resistance and razing rules still apply. The Roost's permanent Culture adds to any Culture supplied by the active Caravansary. A city with three Medieval routes receives +6 Science/+6 Culture from the UA; at Renaissance it receives +9/+9. A Roost adds its permanent +1 Culture and, with qualifying trade, +2 Production separately.

Explore early to find safe caravan destinations. Put several routes in a developed origin to multiply Science and Culture, or spread them among Roost cities for several Production bonuses. Domestic food and Production routes also earn the UA. Favor Science, then Culture and Diplomacy; protect your caravan roads.

Coo has high land-trade, origin, recon, Science, Culture, commerce and diplomacy flavors, friendly approaches and moderate defensive priorities. The normal engine chooses trade routes and production; no custom AI planner claims to understand the Lua yields. Trade disruption uses ordinary CP diplomacy penalties; there is no extra custom hostility ability.

Build with the root [instructions](../README.md). Install the version 5 ZIP, enable CP and this collection, then start a **new game** as Coo. Normal AI opponents receive the same bonuses. Multiplayer/hotseat/Mac remain disabled at the collection level. Pigeon yields are reconstructed from native routes after load without saved Lua state; native save/load verification remains outstanding.
